# Trend Analysis Report

## Objective

Quantify long-term global warming using linear regression.

## Findings

- Regression slope is positive, indicating an overall warming trend.
- Correlation between year and temperature anomaly is strong and positive.
- The p-value is below 0.05, confirming that the trend is statistically significant.
- Decadal averages show a gradual increase in temperature anomalies over time.

## Business / Research Interpretation

The analysis provides quantitative evidence of a long-term increase in global temperature anomalies. This demonstrates how statistical techniques such as regression and decadal aggregation can be used to identify and communicate climate trends.
