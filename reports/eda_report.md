NASA GISS Global Temperature Dataset

## Key Findings

- Global temperature anomaly shows a long-term increasing trend.
- Recent decades contain most of the warmest years.
- Temperature anomalies have shifted from predominantly negative values to positive values.
- Distribution indicates sustained warming over time.
