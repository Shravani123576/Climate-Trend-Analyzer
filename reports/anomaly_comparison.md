# Multi-Method Anomaly Detection

## Objective

Improve anomaly detection reliability by comparing two statistical methods.

## Methods

- Z-Score Detection
- IQR Detection

## Findings

- Years identified by both methods are considered high-confidence anomalies.
- IQR identified additional candidate anomalies not detected by the Z-score approach.
- The comparison demonstrates that different statistical techniques may identify different types of unusual observations.

## Conclusion

Using multiple detection methods increases confidence in anomaly identification and reduces dependence on a single statistical assumption.
