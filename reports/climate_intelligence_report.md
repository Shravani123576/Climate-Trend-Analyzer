# Climate Intelligence Report

## Key Findings

- Long-term warming trend is evident across the historical record.
- Recent decades exhibit the highest average temperature anomalies.
- Several years established new record-high temperature anomalies.
- Cumulative temperature anomalies demonstrate sustained warming over time.
- Year-over-year changes reveal periods of accelerated warming and temporary cooling.

## Business Interpretation

These engineered climate indicators provide a richer understanding than annual values alone. They help identify long-term trends, periods of rapid change, and record-breaking events, making the analysis more useful for decision-makers and future forecasting.
